    Fast Files

    A little less waiting. A lot more finding.

    A local macOS viewer for quickly skimming images, text, and code—and collecting the files worth revisiting.

    Created by Raphael Rouvinov / raphaelrk.

    Browse your way

    Hover to preview. Click to hold a selection; click the selected row again to release it. Scroll in the narrow gutter on the right to keep the preview steady. Drag column headers to arrange the list.
